One13Tech provides professional database administration and consulting services to help organizations manage, maintain, and optimize their critical database environments.

We support businesses throughout the database lifecycle, from architecture planning and installation to administration, troubleshooting, maintenance, and operational improvement. Our focus is on database reliability, performance, security, scalability, and operational efficiency.

## Database Administration Services

Effective database administration is essential for maintaining application availability, data integrity, and business continuity.

Our database administration capabilities cover:

- Database architecture assessment and planning
- Database installation, configuration, and upgrades
- Database health checks and proactive monitoring
- Database user, role, and privilege management
- Backup and recovery configuration
- Replication and cluster administration
- Database maintenance and operational troubleshooting
- Storage, capacity, and resource utilization planning
- Database patching and version management
- Database incident investigation and resolution
- Database documentation and operational best practices

## Database Technologies We Support

We provide database administration and consulting across multiple enterprise database platforms. Our technology coverage includes:

- **MongoDB:** Replica sets, sharded clusters, administration, monitoring, security, and performance tuning.
- **PostgreSQL:** Installation, configuration, replication, maintenance, troubleshooting, and performance optimization.
- **Sybase ASE:** Database administration, backup and recovery, performance tuning, and operational support.
- **IBM DB2:** Database configuration, administration, maintenance, monitoring, and troubleshooting.
- **Microsoft SQL Server:** Database administration, security, backup, recovery, and performance management.
- **Greenplum:** Distributed database administration, segment management, monitoring, and performance optimization.
- **Apache Cloudberry:** Cluster administration, migration support, configuration, and distributed database operations.
- **MySQL & MariaDB:** Database administration, replication, backup, recovery, configuration, and performance tuning.
