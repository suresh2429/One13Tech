One13Tech provides practical database training and knowledge development programs for IT professionals, database administrators, developers, and organizations looking to strengthen their database capabilities.

Our training approach combines technical concepts, real-world scenarios, hands-on exercises, and operational best practices to help participants develop practical database administration and management skills.

## Database Training Programs

We offer training across multiple database technologies, with content tailored to the experience level and learning requirements of participants.

Training areas include:

- MongoDB Administration
- PostgreSQL Administration
- Sybase ASE Administration
- IBM DB2 Administration
- Microsoft SQL Server Administration
- Greenplum & Apache Cloudberry
- MySQL & MariaDB Administration
- Database Performance Tuning
- Backup, Recovery & High Availability
- Database Security & Troubleshooting

## Practical Learning Approach
