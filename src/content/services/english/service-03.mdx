One13Tech helps organizations design and maintain highly available database environments with reliable backup, recovery, replication, and disaster recovery strategies.

Our approach focuses on minimizing service disruption, protecting critical business data, and improving database recovery readiness through structured planning, implementation, and validation.

## High Availability Solutions

High availability is essential for applications that depend on continuous database access. We help organizations assess database architecture and implement suitable availability solutions based on workload requirements and recovery objectives.

Our capabilities include:

- Database replication and cluster configuration
- MongoDB replica sets and sharded cluster availability
- PostgreSQL streaming replication
- Database failover planning and configuration
- Primary and standby database architecture
- Database health monitoring
- High availability architecture assessment
- Failover testing and validation

## Backup & Disaster Recovery

A reliable disaster recovery strategy helps organizations restore critical database services following infrastructure failures, data corruption, or unexpected incidents.

We help develop backup and recovery approaches that align with business continuity requirements.

Our services include:

- Database backup strategy and configuration
- Full, incremental, and point-in-time recovery planning
- Backup retention and storage management
- Backup validation and restore testing
- Disaster recovery architecture planning
- Recovery Time Objective (RTO) and Recovery Point Objective (RPO) assessment
- Disaster recovery procedures and documentation

## Business Continuity & Recovery Readiness

High availability and disaster recovery require regular validation, clear operational procedures, and a well-understood recovery process.
